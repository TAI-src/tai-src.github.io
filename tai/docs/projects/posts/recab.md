# RECAB: Reducing Energy Consumption in Algorithm Benchmarking

Compare algorithms sustainably: Stop recomputing the same results!

<!-- more -->

Optimisation methods help to solve many real-world problems, ranging from finding the fastest train routes to choosing the best material for solar cells. In algorithms research, such methods must be scientifically tested before they can be used in practice. These tests often consume large amounts of energy, as they involve running thousands of expensive simulations on high-performance computing clusters. We propose a tool that stores and reuses simulation results to avoid unnecessary re-execution. This can significantly reduce energy consumption and shorten the time researchers need to obtain results.
